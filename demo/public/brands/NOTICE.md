# Gauge demo brand assets

The six SVG marks (`aster`, `lucent`, `harbor`, `delta`, `mosaic`, `common`) are original fictional identities for synthetic demonstration companies. They do not represent real startups.

The following website icons were downloaded from assets linked by the companies' official websites on September 25, 2026. They are used to identify the corresponding public company profiles. Trademarks and copyrights remain with their respective owners; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.
